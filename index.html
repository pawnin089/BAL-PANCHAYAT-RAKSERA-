Bal Panchayat Raksera (BPR) is a child-led community initiative where children come together to learn, share ideas, take responsibility and work for a better community.

About BPR

A platform built around children's voices.

BPR encourages participation, learning, teamwork and leadership through activities planned and carried out with children.

Our vision

To create a community where every child gets opportunities to learn, express themselves, take part in decisions and contribute positively.

“When children participate, communities become stronger.”

Our committees

Children turning ideas into action.

BPR works through focused committees so children can take responsibility for different areas of community life.

01

Academic Committee

Focuses on learning, communication and study support. A key goal is to build a permanent library, starting with a jhola library.

02

Sports Committee

Promotes sports, teamwork and regular activities, including efforts to make kabaddi work permanently and properly.

03

Culture Committee

Encourages creativity, local culture, expression and activities that bring children together.

04

Environment Committee

Builds awareness and action around a cleaner, greener and healthier community.
